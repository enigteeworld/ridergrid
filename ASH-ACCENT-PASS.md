# Dispatch NG ash/slate accent pass

This pass keeps emerald green as the primary Dispatch NG action/trust colour and introduces cool ash/slate as the secondary visual family.

Changes include:
- teal decorative gradients replaced with slate/ash accents across customer, rider, admin, auth and public surfaces;
- customer dashboard hero and protection surfaces neutralized to reduce the green wash;
- role badge changed to a neutral ash/slate treatment;
- global secondary/accent/muted design tokens shifted toward cool slate;
- semantic green states (online, verified, success, escrow/security) remain green;
- destructive/warning/status colours remain semantic and were not flattened into the brand palette.

No SQL changes are required.
